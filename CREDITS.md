# Credits

All third-party assets bundled with Modulo are **CC0 (public domain)**. Thank you to the artists and libraries who make them freely available.

- Textures, skies and 3D models: [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) (CC0) and [ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com) (CC0).
- Water normal map: from the [three.js](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js) examples (MIT).

Assets are fetched and optimised by `scripts/fetch-textures.py`, `scripts/fetch-skies.py` and `scripts/fetch-models.mjs` (run behind a proxy with `NODE_USE_ENV_PROXY=1`).
